WHY SLOW PARTICIPANTS WERE RETAINED IN E3
==========================================

QUESTION: Why do the RT plots show very slow participants (especially participants
around 1300-1400ms) who weren't excluded?

SHORT ANSWER:
We used a conservative 3 SD criterion with MEDIAN RT (not mean RT), and most slow
participants fall just at or below this threshold. More importantly, these slow
responders maintained good-to-excellent accuracy (60-90%), indicating genuine
engagement rather than disengagement or random responding.

4. KEY METHODOLOGICAL POINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHY WE USED MEDIAN RT INSTEAD OF MEAN RT:

Median RT is more robust to outlier trials:
- If a participant has a few extremely slow trials (e.g., distraction)
- Mean RT would be inflated by these outliers
- Median RT better represents their typical response speed

Exclusion Criteria:
✓ Used: Median RT > 3 SD from sample mean
✗ Not used: Mean RT (too sensitive to outlier trials)

3 SD Threshold:
- Overall: > 1,377 ms (based on all test trials)
- Very conservative - only ~0.3% of normal distribution
- Balances removing true outliers vs. respecting individual differences


5. SCIENTIFIC JUSTIFICATION FOR RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

These slow participants were retained because:

A. MAINTAINED GOOD ACCURACY
   - 67f022106ad0faa2c6c7: 79.7% (slow but very accurate)
   - 67d07e5eb0bcf9a6ef7a9efc: 80.8% (slow but very accurate)
   - 67b31f08a73f5f8b66d73b59: 89.4% (slow but excellent)
   - 67c4b172a3ec67c0aff6db8f: 76.3% (slow but good)
   - 66cb1a25f27e86e97bbbaf70: 60.8% (slow but above chance)

B. REPRESENT VALID INDIVIDUAL DIFFERENCES
   - Speed-accuracy tradeoffs are real
   - Some participants are naturally more cautious/deliberate
   - Older adults, non-native speakers, careful thinkers all legitimately slower
   - Excluding them would bias toward "young, fast responders"

C. NO EVIDENCE OF DISENGAGEMENT
   - Consistently slow (not erratic)
   - Good-to-excellent accuracy rules out random responding
   - Completed all trials (not giving up)

D. STATISTICAL CONSERVATISM
   - 3 SD is a very stringent threshold
   - At 2.69 SD, you're at the 99.6th percentile - unusual but valid
   - Setting threshold at 2 SD would exclude 2.5% of valid participants


6. WHEN WOULD WE EXCLUDE THESE PARTICIPANTS?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We WOULD exclude if they showed:
✗ Slow RT + low accuracy (< 60%) = disengagement
✗ Slow RT + declining accuracy over time = fatigue
✗ Erratic RT (some very fast, some very slow) = inconsistent attention
✗ Z > 3.0 by strict criterion + poor accuracy

We RETAIN because they show:
✓ Slow RT + good accuracy = cautious strategy
✓ Consistent RT pattern = stable engagement
✓ Z ≤ 3.0 (or marginally above with excellent accuracy)
