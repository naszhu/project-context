Figure B4
Experiment 1: Final-test order effects for response time.
Note. The left column (a) shows results by final-test order, with 420 final-test trials grouped into 10 temporal bins. The right column (b) shows results by initial-list order. Each row corresponds to a different final-test condition: backward, forward, and random, as labeled. In the backward condition (top row), the second column reverses the first; in the forward condition (middle row), the second column repeats the first. Blue squares = ST; green circles = SO; orange triangles = TO; red crosses = FTO. Response time represents the mean of median RT across participants. Error ribbons represent ±1 SEM.

Figure B5
Experiment 1: Individual differences in response time for initial and final tests.
Note. Panel B5.1 = participant mean RT during the initial test (ordered from fastest to slowest on the x-axis); panel B5.2 = mean RT during the final test (ordered from fastest to slowest on the x-axis). Response time represents the mean RT across all trials for each participant.
